📘 Financial Guide

Simple steps to manage your income and expenses

A budget is a plan for your money. Use this guide as a starting point, then adjust it to fit your actual needs and situation.

1. Manage your income

  • Record money you receive, such as allowance, salary, gifts, or side-job earnings.
  • Write the date, amount, and where it came from.
  • Plan how much to use for needs, wants, and savings before spending.

2. Manage your expenses

  • Record each purchase as soon as you can.
  • Choose a category such as food, transportation, school, or entertainment.
  • Check your spending regularly and look for costs you can reduce.

3. Try the 50/30/20 rule

A simple budgeting guide for your take-home income:

50%Needs
30%Wants
20%Savings

Needs include essentials. Wants are optional extras. Savings can go toward goals or emergencies. These percentages are a guide, not a strict rule.

4. Build a saving habit

  • Set a clear, realistic savings goal.
  • Save a small amount regularly, even if it changes from week to week.
  • Before buying something optional, pause and ask if it fits your budget.
Example: If you receive ₱1,000
₱500 for needs + ₱300 for wants + ₱200 for savings = ₱1,000. Adjust the amounts if your essential expenses are higher. Never skip important needs just to follow a percentage.
